About Poolentra.

Poolentra is Australian pool service software from a team with years of experience running online businesses, built around the working day of pool-service owners and their teams.

Our focus

Built around your working day.

Your customers, jobs, team, stock and admin should be easier to keep track of—even when you’re out servicing pools.

Our experience

Years of online business, focused on pool service.

We’ve spent years building and running online businesses. That taught us what makes software worth using every day: it has to be quick, clear and dependable, and it has to save time rather than add admin.

Poolentra puts that experience into one focus: the working day of an Australian pool-service business, from the first enquiry to the regular run, the visit, the water test and the invoice.
