Accessibility

We want the Poolentra website to be easy to use for everyone, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader or zoom.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

What we’ve done

  • Clear page structure and descriptive headings.
  • Links and buttons that say what they do.
  • Navigation, menus and questions that work with a keyboard.
  • Visible keyboard focus.
  • Pages that fit small screens and enlarged text.
  • Less motion for visitors who ask their device for it.
  • Plain language and descriptive alternative text for images.

We’ve checked every page with automated accessibility tests against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 level AA, and fixed what they found.

If something is hard to use

Email support@poolentra.com and tell us which page and what went wrong. We’ll reply within 5 business days, and we can send you the information another way.
