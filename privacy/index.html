Privacy Policy

How Poolentra collects, uses, stores and shares personal information.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who we are

Poolentra is a business name of Camper Travel Pty Ltd (ABN 43 120 480 962). “We”, “us” and “Poolentra” mean Camper Travel Pty Ltd trading as Poolentra. We make pool service software for Australian pool businesses.

This policy explains how we handle personal information. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Privacy contact: manager@poolentra.com

Who this policy covers

  • people who visit poolentra.com.au
  • people who email us, call us or book a demo
  • people at pool businesses we contact about Poolentra
  • staff of businesses that use Poolentra
  • people whose details a pool business keeps in Poolentra, such as its customers

What we collect

When you visit our website. You don’t need to tell us who you are. Google Analytics records how the site is used: pages viewed, buttons and links clicked, device and browser type, approximate location and how you found us. GitHub, which hosts the website, records IP addresses for security.

When you contact us or book a demo. Your name, email, phone, business name and time zone, the appointment you choose, and any optional answers about your business, such as how many technicians you have, what software you use now and what you’d like help with. We keep the emails you send us and our replies.

When we contact pool businesses. Business contact details (business name, the contact person’s name, work email and phone, website and location) and notes of our conversations, demos and what the business is interested in.

When your business uses Poolentra. For staff: name, work email, role, sign-in details and a record of changes made in Poolentra. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and confirms it’s verified. For the business’s customers: names, addresses, phone numbers and email addresses, site access details (such as gate codes and where keys are kept), pool and equipment details, photos taken at visits, visit and water-test history, quotes and invoices, and emails in a mailbox the business connects to Poolentra. If a business connects its Google Business Profile: the reviews its customers post there (the reviewer’s name, rating and words) and the business’s replies.

When phone features are turned on. When you call us, or call a business that uses Poolentra’s phone features: your phone number, a recording and transcript of the call, and what the call was about.

We don’t ask for sensitive information such as health details. Please don’t send it to us.

How we collect it

  • From you: when you fill in a form, email or call us, or talk to us in a demo.
  • Automatically: through Google Analytics cookies on our website.
  • From public sources: business websites and online business listings, when we look for pool businesses that may want Poolentra.
  • From the business you work for or deal with: when it sets up your account or keeps your details in Poolentra, or connects a service such as Xero or its Outlook mailbox.

Why we use it

  • to answer your enquiry and arrange your demo, including the calendar invitation, confirmation and reminder
  • to follow up with pool businesses about Poolentra, and with customers about their account and new features
  • to answer and follow up phone calls, when phone features are turned on
  • to help a business read and reply to its Google reviews, when it connects its Business Profile
  • to provide, support, secure and improve Poolentra
  • to see how our website is used so we can improve it
  • to bill subscribing businesses and meet our legal obligations

If you don’t give us your details

To book a demo we need your name, email, phone and business name. The questions about your technicians, the software you use now and what you’d like help with are optional. If you don’t give us the details we need, we may not be able to help. You can ask a general question without giving your name.

Marketing

We only send marketing emails or texts when you’ve agreed or the Spam Act 2003 allows it. Every marketing message tells you how to opt out. Booking a demo doesn’t sign you up to marketing. Messages about a demo, your account or your service aren’t marketing.

If you ask us to stop contacting you, we add your business name and email to our do-not-contact list, so we don’t contact you again.

Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics uses cookies. We never send it your name, email, phone, business name or anything you type into a form, and its advertising features are off. You can block cookies in your browser or use Google’s opt-out add-on. The website works without them.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell personal information. We share it only with:

  • providers who help us run Poolentra: Google (email, calendar, Meet, sign-in and analytics; Maps, which receives a pool’s address when a technician opens directions; and Business Profile, when a business connects it, to read its reviews and post the replies it approves), GitHub (website hosting), Sliplane and Hetzner (app and database hosting), Xero (when a business connects its Xero account), Microsoft (when a business connects its Outlook mailbox, so Poolentra can show its emails and send replies it approves), Telnyx (phone calls and text messages, including call recordings and transcripts, when those features are turned on), and OpenAI and Anthropic (AI features in the app, which receive only the information needed for the task)
  • our professional advisers, such as our accountant or lawyer, when needed
  • anyone the law requires us to share it with
  • a buyer of our business, who must keep handling it under this policy

Information stored outside Australia

Some of these providers store or process information overseas. Our app and database servers are in Finland. Google, GitHub, Xero, OpenAI and Anthropic store or process information mainly in the United States. Google, Microsoft and Telnyx may also process information in the United States and other countries where they have data centres. We choose providers with strong security and privacy commitments.

How we protect it

  • the website and app use encrypted (https) connections
  • demo booking details are stored encrypted
  • only approved people can sign in to Poolentra, and each business’s records are kept separate from every other business’s
  • the technician app keeps the day’s visit details, including site access details, on the technician’s phone, so it works without signal
  • our team accesses personal information only when their work needs it

If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we’ll tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

How long we keep it

  • Sales and demo records: we keep business contact details and our notes while they’re useful to us, including for future offers to our customers. If you ask us to stop contacting you, we stop, and add your business name and email to our do-not-contact list so it stays that way.
  • Website analytics: Google Analytics keeps visit data for up to 14 months.
  • Businesses that use Poolentra: we keep a business’s records while it uses Poolentra, then return or delete them as agreed with that business, except where the law requires us to keep them (such as tax records).

Accessing or correcting your information

Email manager@poolentra.com to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or to correct it. We’ll check who you are and reply within 30 days. It’s free to ask. If we can’t do what you ask, we’ll tell you why and how to complain.

If your details are kept in Poolentra by a pool business you deal with, ask that business first. That business controls those records, and we’ll help it respond.

Complaints

Email manager@poolentra.com with what happened. We’ll acknowledge your complaint within 5 business days, look into it and reply within 30 days.

If you’re not happy with our answer, you can complain to the OAIC at www.oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Automated decisions

We don’t use computer programs to make decisions about you that could significantly affect your rights or interests. Poolentra’s AI features suggest answers and drafts, and a person decides what happens. If that changes, we’ll update this policy first.

Changes to this policy

We’ll post any change here with a new date. If a change significantly affects businesses that use Poolentra, we’ll email them.
