Who is Poolentra for?

If you run a pool-service business on your own or with a small team in Australia, Poolentra is built for you.

Is Poolentra only for large companies?

No. You can be a solo operator or run a small team. The aim is to help you keep track of your work as your business grows.

Does Poolentra replace the owner or the team?

No. You and your team stay in charge. The aim is to give you clearer information so you can decide what to do next.
