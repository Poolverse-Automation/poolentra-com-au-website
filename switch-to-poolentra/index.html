Questions

Switching questions, answered.

What does the import include?

Your customers and pools from your existing software. Common pool software exports are matched column by column automatically, and your regular services are placed on your runs, so you don’t re-enter them by hand.

Can my prices come across?

Yes. If you invoice through Xero, your clients’ prices can be taken from your Xero invoice lines.

What do you need from us?

An export of your customers and pools from your current software, and access to Xero if you use it. We’ll agree the details and training with you before you start.

What if my data needs extra clean-up?

We’ll tell you what’s involved before any unusual clean-up starts.

Will Poolentra replace the software I already use?

We’ll start with the tools you already use. Together, we’ll check what can connect, what needs a manual step and whether a change makes sense for you.

Does it connect to my accounting software?

Tell us which tools you use. We’ll check compatibility, permissions and limitations with you. We’ll agree the connections needed for your setup.

What would getting started involve?

Start with your team, your current tools and one task you want to improve. Before you begin, we’ll agree on data transfer, setup, training, support and costs with you.
